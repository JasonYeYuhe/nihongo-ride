FAQ

What is Nihongo Ride? A typing-practice app for people learning Japanese (JLPT N5–N1). You advance across Japan by typing kana correctly, learning vocabulary and real sentences as you go.
Do I need a Japanese keyboard or IME? No. Just type romaji on your normal keyboard — the app has its own built-in matcher that converts your keystrokes to kana in real time, accepting every valid spelling (shi/si, tsu/tu, n/nn, and so on).
What are the game modes?
  • Journey — an immersive ride across Japan; type to keep moving.
  • Time Attack — 60 seconds, as many words as you can.
  • Practice — a calm, distraction-free mode for words or long passages, with an optional BLIND challenge (no romaji hints).
How does the review system work? Words you mistype flow into a spaced-repetition queue based on the SM-2 algorithm. The app brings them back at increasing intervals so you reinforce exactly what you struggle with.
Can I turn the hints off? Yes. Romaji hints are a toggle. Turn them off in Practice mode for a true BLIND typing challenge.
Does it work offline? Everything you practise works with no connection at all — on a plane, in a cafe, anywhere. Optional extras use Apple's own services under your Apple Account: iCloud sync (on by default, switchable off in Settings) keeps your progress in step across your devices, Game Center receives Time Attack scores and achievements if you are signed in, and any optional purchase goes through the App Store. There is no server of ours, and the developer receives nothing. (See the Privacy Policy.)
Where is my progress saved? Locally on your device, inside the app's sandbox container. With iCloud sync on, your review schedule, ride log, saved words and word lists are also in your own private iCloud — nowhere else, and never anywhere the developer can read. (See the Privacy Policy.)
The meanings or readings look off for a word. Some content was machine-assisted during development and is being reviewed by native speakers. If you spot something wrong, email us the word and we'll fix it fast.

常见问题

Nihongo Ride 是什么? 一款给日语学习者(JLPT N5–N1)的打字练习应用。正确打出假名,你就沿着日本一路前进,同时学到词汇和真实例句。
需要日文键盘或输入法吗? 不需要。用平常的键盘打罗马字即可,应用内置的匹配器会实时把按键转换成假名,所有合法拼法都接受(shi/si、tsu/tu、n/nn 等)。
能离线使用吗? 所有练习完全离线可用。可选的附加功能用的是 Apple 自己的服务,走你自己的 Apple 账户:iCloud 同步(默认开启,可在设置中关闭)让多台设备进度一致;登录 Game Center 时会提交限时模式成绩和成就;任何可选内购都通过 App Store 完成。我们没有自己的服务器,开发者收不到任何数据。(见隐私政策。)
进度保存在哪里? 保存在你设备本地的应用沙盒容器里。开启 iCloud 同步后,复习计划、骑行记录、收藏词和词单也会存在你自己的私有 iCloud 里,别处都没有,开发者也无法读取。(见隐私政策。)
某个词的释义或读音好像不对。 部分内容在开发时借助了机器,正在逐步人工校对。发现错误请把那个词发邮件给我们,我们会尽快修正。
